UR GROZY 🛒

UR GROZY is a hyperlocal grocery delivery platform that connects customers with nearby local grocery stores and delivery partners through a single digital platform.

Instead of forcing customers to order from large centralized marketplaces, UR GROZY helps them shop from their preferred local grocery store while giving local shopkeepers and riders access to a digital ordering and delivery system.

---

🚀 What Problem Does UR GROZY Solve?

Many local grocery stores still depend on:

- Phone calls and WhatsApp orders
- Manual order management
- Limited online visibility
- Customers visiting the store physically
- Separate arrangements for delivery
- Difficulty managing delivery partners

Customers also face problems such as:

- Not knowing which nearby stores are available
- Difficulty finding products from their preferred local shop
- No simple way to place grocery orders online
- Limited visibility of order status
- Lack of convenient local delivery

UR GROZY brings customers, local stores, and delivery riders together in one platform.

---

💡 How UR GROZY Works

👤 Customer

Customers can:

1. Open UR GROZY
2. Find nearby grocery stores
3. Select a preferred store or shop from available stores
4. Browse products or submit a grocery list/image
5. Place an order
6. Track the order
7. Receive the order through a delivery partner

---

🏪 Shopkeeper

Shopkeepers can:

- Register their store
- Manage their store profile
- Manage products and prices
- Receive customer orders
- Accept or reject orders
- Prepare orders
- Request/coordinate delivery
- Manage their store availability

---

🛵 Rider

Delivery partners can:

- Register as riders
- Submit required information for verification
- Wait for admin approval
- Go online/offline
- Receive nearby delivery requests
- Accept or reject delivery requests
- Pick up orders from stores
- Deliver orders to customers
- Update delivery status
- View delivery history and earnings

---

🛡️ Admin

The administration system provides centralized control over the platform.

Admins can:

- Manage customers
- Manage stores
- Approve/reject store registrations
- Approve/reject rider applications
- Manage riders
- Manage the global product catalog
- Monitor orders
- Manage platform operations
- Monitor the overall marketplace

🌍 Hyperlocal Approach

UR GROZY focuses on local commerce.

For example, if a customer is located in Chikkamagaluru, the platform can prioritize stores operating in that locality.

This allows customers to discover and order from nearby businesses instead of depending only on large centralized grocery platforms.

---

🧠 Global Product Catalog

UR GROZY can maintain a common product catalog containing products such as:

- Rice
- Sugar
- Cooking oil
- Milk
- Biscuits
- Snacks
- Household products
- Personal-care products
- Other grocery essentials

Individual stores can associate products from the global catalog with their own inventory and set their own prices and availability.

This avoids unnecessarily duplicating the same product information for every store.

                       │

       │               │               │
   Customer        Partner          Admin
     Portal         Portal          Portal
                       │
                ┌──────┴──────┐
